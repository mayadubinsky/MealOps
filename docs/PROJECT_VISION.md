MealOps uses AI to create a personalized weekly plan based on the user's dietary restrictions and preferences. 

The app saves users time and mental effort by reducing the need to search for recipes and plan healthy meals.
Instead of spending hours deciding what to prepare, users can focus on cooking and enjoying their food. 
MealOps will also generate a consolidated shopping list and provide reminders for meal preparation.
The initial version will support a single user with one set of dietary restrictions and food preferences.
